# Code of Conduct

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone. This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, discussions, pull requests, and community events.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language and be considerate of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Show empathy and respect in all interactions, especially when disagreements arise.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism and offer it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to **community@ascia.example.com** with as much detail as possible. Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Enforcement Process
- Maintainers will review the report promptly and acknowledge receipt within five business days.
- After review, maintainers may issue warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on severity.
- Appeals can be submitted to the same contact email and will be reviewed by a different maintainer when possible.
